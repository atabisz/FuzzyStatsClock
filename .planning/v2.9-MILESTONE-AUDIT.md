## Tech Debt

### Phase 35: process-count-threshold

1. **Deferred display refresh** (`MainWindow.xaml.cs:577-581`) — `SetProcessThreshold()` updates `_processCountThreshold` and calls `SaveSettings()` but does not call `UpdateUptimeDisplay()` or restart the stats timer. The visual count change is deferred to the next timer tick (1–10s depending on configured interval). Compare: `SetStatsInterval()` restarts the timer immediately. Fix: add `UpdateStatsDisplay()` or equivalent at end of `SetProcessThreshold()`.

2. **Stale inline comment** (`MainWindow.xaml.cs:470`) — Comment reads "Count processes with >= 5% CPU utilization" but the threshold is now configurable. Should say "Count processes at or above the configured threshold (_processCountThreshold)".
