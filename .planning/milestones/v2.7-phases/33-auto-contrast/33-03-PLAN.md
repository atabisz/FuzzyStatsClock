    - 500ms sampler DispatcherTimer in MainWindow
    - Tray "Auto-Contrast" toggle item (off by default)
    - Pause on ghost mode, pause on opacity=0, freeze on drag
    - ResetToDefaults disables auto-contrast
    - AutoContrastEnabled persisted to settings.json

    Run the app (dotnet run --project FuzzyClock.App or launch the built exe).

    **CONTRAST-01: Tray toggle + persistence**
    1. Right-click tray icon — confirm "Auto-Contrast" item is present and UNCHECKED (default off)
    2. Click "Auto-Contrast" — item becomes CHECKED; feature is now active
    3. Quit the app; relaunch — right-click tray → "Auto-Contrast" should be CHECKED (persisted)
    4. Click "Auto-Contrast" again to disable — item becomes UNCHECKED
    5. Quit and relaunch — "Auto-Contrast" should be UNCHECKED (persisted as off)

    **CONTRAST-02: Override on light background**
    6. Enable Auto-Contrast via tray

    8. Within ~1 second: the widget text should change from white (or your accent color) to a darker color
    9. Confirm the text is now readable against the white background

    **CONTRAST-03: Restore accent on dark background**
    10. With Auto-Contrast enabled, drag the widget over a dark area of the screen (dark taskbar, dark terminal, black wallpaper)
    11. Within ~1.5 seconds (hysteresis): text should restore to the configured accent color
    12. Expected: no rapid flickering on the boundary between light and dark areas

    **CONTRAST-04: Disabled always shows accent**
    13. Disable Auto-Contrast via tray
    14. Place widget over a white background — text should remain in the accent color (white or preset), even if it's unreadable

    **Drag freeze**
    15. Enable Auto-Contrast; hold left mouse button and drag the widget — confirm text color does NOT change during drag
    16. Release mouse — text color may update on next 500ms tick

    **Ghost mode pause**
    17. Enable Auto-Contrast; hover over the widget WITHOUT Ctrl+Alt held — widget goes ghost (Opacity=0)
    18. While invisible: confirm no errors; ghost restore timer should still work
    19. Hold Ctrl+Alt and hover — normal hover (not ghost); auto-contrast should continue working

    **Reset to Defaults**
    20. Enable Auto-Contrast; select "Reset to Defaults" in tray — confirm Auto-Contrast is now UNCHECKED and text is back to accent color
